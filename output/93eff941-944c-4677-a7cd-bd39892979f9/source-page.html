Italian Animal Alphabet Abacus | Wooden Italian Learning Toy for Kids

$25.99
 
 
Share:

Introduce children to Italian letters, animal words, numbers, and counting with this cheerful Italian Animal Alphabet Abacus. Made with a sturdy wooden frame and colorful flip-and-slide blocks, this hands-on learning toy is a fun way for little ones to begin exploring the Italian language through play.

The abacus features 30 natural wood blocks that children can flip, slide, and turn along the wooden rods. One side includes the traditional 21-letter Italian alphabet along with numbers 1 through 9. The other side features colorful animal pictures with their Italian names, plus counting dots to help children practice early number recognition.

This Italian learning toy encourages fine motor skills, hand-eye coordination, letter recognition, animal vocabulary, counting, and early language learning. It is a sweet educational gift for children, grandchildren, Italian-American families, bilingual homes, Montessori-inspired playrooms, and anyone who wants to introduce Italian in a simple, playful way.

The wooden bear side panels are inspired by our signature Italian-speaking plush bears, Giovanni and Giuliana, adding a special Italian Children’s Market touch to this adorable learning toy.
